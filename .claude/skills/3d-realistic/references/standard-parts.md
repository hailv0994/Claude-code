## Everyday industrial

- DIN rail TS35: 35 wide × 7.5 high, 1 mm sheet.
- Pneumatic tube: Ø4 (ID 2.5), Ø6 (ID 4), Ø8 (ID 5), Ø10 (ID 6.5), Ø12 (ID 8).
- Push-in fitting body: roughly 2× the tube Ø in width, 20–30 long, hex boss at the thread.
- Conveyor roller: Ø50 / Ø60 / Ø76 steel, wall 1.5–2.
- Caster: Ø75 / Ø100 / Ø125 wheel, plate 100 × 85 with 4 × Ø9 holes.
- Euro pallet 1200 × 800 × 144; plastic bin 600 × 400 × 230 / 300.
- Human reference: 1680 tall (JP/VN average), shoulder 440, working height 900–1000.
- Door 900 × 2100, standard factory bay column pitch 6000–9000, gantry clearance 4000.

## Catalogue parts that ALWAYS need the scout

SMC / Festo / CKD cylinders and valves · THK / NSK / HIWIN rails and ball screws · servo and stepper motors · gearboxes · Keyence / Omron / SICK sensors · robots (Fanuc, Yaskawa, UR) · grippers · any machine model number. These have model-specific dimensions; using "about right" numbers for them is the single most common way these models get rejected.
